    Welcome to the 3D Box Editor

    Place 3D boxes around objects in your image, then drag, rotate, scale, or rearrange them. Export the scene as JSON to drive the editing model.

    • ▢ Boxes drag / scale / rotate
    • ▦ Floor use the bottom-right bar
    • 📷 Camera orbit + pan the scene
    • 📁 Import / Export in the left rail

    Tap the ? button on the left rail any time to view all keyboard shortcuts.
